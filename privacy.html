To Himself

Privacy policy

To Himself collects no data.

The app has no account and no server, and it contains no advertising and no analytics software.

What stays on your phone

What you set, save or write stays on your device: the theme, the text size, the appearance, your saved passages, the time of the daily passage, the looks you chose, the days you have read on a reading path and your journal. The notifications are scheduled on the device itself. Your journal leaves the app only if you export it, and then it goes where you send it.

Premium

On an iPhone, a subscription is bought from Apple, with your Apple account, and Apple handles the payment. On Android, it is bought from Google Play, with your Google account, and Google handles the payment. The app learns from your phone only whether the subscription is running. It keeps that answer on the device and sends it nowhere.

Sharing a passage

When you share a passage, the picture goes only to the app you choose. If you save it to your photos, the app adds that one picture. It cannot see your photo library.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you open one of its pages, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes; its privacy statement says more. The pages themselves set no cookies and contain no tracking.
