To Himself

Support

To Himself is an iPhone app. It shows passages from Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus and Seneca one at a time, in clear modern English, with the classic translation one tap away.

Contact

A question, a mistake in a passage, or something that does not work: write to jajuhelp@gmail.com.

Privacy

To Himself collects no data. The privacy policy says what stays on your iPhone.
